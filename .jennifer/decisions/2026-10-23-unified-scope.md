# Decision · Unify planner and tracker into one product

_2026-10-23 · Decided by: founder · Recorded by: Jennifer_

**Decision.** skkil-time becomes one loop: Plan (weekly outcomes, tasks, time blocks) → Do (focus/supervise/review blocks) → Capture (passive desktop + agent hooks) → Review (felt vs shipped, verdicts). The 10-18 tracker becomes the reality layer.

**Why.** No product combines planning, passive capture and agent awareness (competitor scan 2026-10-23). The founder's goal is to fix AI's "feels productive, isn't" problem, which needs intention and verified outcome side by side.

**Alternatives rejected.** Planner first with the tracker later (loses the only wedge; MCP alone is table stakes). Replace the tracker with a planner (enters the crowded $4-7 list market with nothing new).

**What we give up.** A smaller first product. Scope risk is mitigated by the ActivityWatch backend, no in-app AI, and Linux/macOS only in P0.

**Reversibility.** High until Phase 1 code starts.

**Revisit if.** Phase 0 interviews show fewer than 4 of 10 feel the "busy but not shipped" pain.
